How Stealth4 protects

Three modules, one agentic brain

Each module answers one question about your risk. HALO correlates the answers and runs the agentic SOC on top.

Exposure

Sightline · WebProbe

What can an attacker reach from outside? Continuously maps your internet-facing attack surface across infrastructure, cloud services and identities, ranked by blast radius.

Posture

M365 & Azure Posture

What can be abused once they are inside? Checks vulnerabilities, configuration and controls against your baseline and NIS2, flags drift, and proposes the fix.

Intelligence

Signals
