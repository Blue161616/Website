# ISOC in Microsoft Defender Explained

An interactive explainer of **Integrated Security Operations (ISOC) in Microsoft
Defender**, announced on 23 September 2026. It is written for two audiences at
once, management and security engineers, and runs entirely in the browser as
static content, with no sign-in and no tenant connection.

- **File:** `isoc-explainer.html`
- **Category:** Security Operations
- **Status:** Explainer (based on preview information, subject to change)

---

## What it does

A **Management / Both / Technical** switch at the top changes the opening text,
benefit cards, pros and cons and the checklist for whoever is reading.

- **Agentic cyber stack:** the six layers (signals, context, actuators, models,
  harness, agents), with Project Perception on top and ISOC as the foundation.
- **Why it exists:** an animated before/after of siloed tools versus one
  platform and one data model.
- **Integrated protection loop:** detect & disrupt, predict, harden, focus.
- **What you get:** SIEM features, 90 days of retention, the $2.40/GB
  third-party meter, cases, natural-language playbooks, data lake and Fabric.
- **How it works:** the Defender data path and the non-Microsoft data path, and
  what works with and without an ISOC workspace.
- **Eligibility checker:** a verdict, the dates that apply and recommended
  actions based on licence and Sentinel usage.
- **Timeline, cost estimator (US list prices), pros/cons/watch-outs, quiz,
  next-steps checklist and resources** (announcement blog, launch event,
  whitepaper).
