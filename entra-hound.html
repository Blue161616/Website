ENTRAHOUND

ENTRA ID · READ-ONLY

Sign in through your own app registration and EntraHound reads the directory the way Graph exposes it: tenant, licences, identities, applications, roles, devices and policies. Nothing is written, nothing leaves the browser.

Step 1

Connect with your app registration

EntraHound uses a single-page-application registration in the tenant you want to read. The page signs you in with delegated permissions, so what it can see is the intersection of the app's consented permissions and your own directory role.

App registration setup
  • Platform: Single-page application, redirect URI exactly https://blue16.nl/entra-hound.html
  • Required delegated permission (admin consent): Directory.Read.All
  • Optional, each lights up one more section: Policy.Read.All (Conditional Access, security defaults, authorization policy), AuditLog.Read.All (last sign-in per user), RoleManagement.Read.Directory (PIM eligible assignments).
  • MSAL cannot run from file://; serve the page over http(s).
