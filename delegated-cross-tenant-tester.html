Delegated Cross-Tenant Tester Lab

Demonstrates that a multi-tenant app with delegated permissions reads the signed-in user's tenant — not the app owner's. Sign in with an account from your other tenant and watch the access-token tid/scp and the live GET /me come from that tenant. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere except Microsoft.

1 · Your multi-tenant app

An app registration you own, Supported account types = multi-tenant, platform SPA with this page's URL as a redirect URI.
organizations routes to whichever account you pick — the easiest way to hop tenants.
Space-separated. User.Read always works. For the clearest "capped by the user" demo, add Policy.Read.All — admins can read Conditional Access policies, but ordinary users can't, even with the scope. Add RoleManagement.Read.Directory and AuditLog.Read.All for the same effect on role assignments and sign-in logs, and Directory.Read.All to read the directory. All the *.Read.All scopes need admin consent in the signed-in user's tenant.
