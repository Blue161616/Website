Delegated Cross-Tenant Tester Lab

Demonstrates that a multi-tenant app with delegated permissions reads the signed-in user's tenant — not the app owner's. Sign in with an account from your other tenant and watch the access-token tid/scp and the live GET /me come from that tenant. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere except Microsoft.
