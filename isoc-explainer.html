01 · Why it exists

The physics of security have changed

Attackers use AI to move at machine speed, SOC tooling is still split across products, and costs keep rising because the same data is stored and paid for several times.

Agents inherit complexity

AI agents dropped into fragmented systems inherit the same silos, gaps and duplicated data as humans do. They can't reason across what they can't see.

Operations stay fragmented

Separate tools, workflows and owners for XDR, SIEM, threat intel, SOAR and ticketing slow down every investigation.

Data gets duplicated

Security data is stored, moved and paid for multiple times across disconnected systems. That's cost without extra protection.

Before: siloed tools, copied data

Every tool keeps its own copy. The analyst stitches the story together by hand.

06 · Cost

What will it cost?

Defender data is included. You pay for non-Microsoft data, retention beyond what's included, and Fabric compute if you use it. Every meter follows the workspace's region: EU +20%, rest of world +30%.
