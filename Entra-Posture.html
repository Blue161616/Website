User Accounts

Security posture·

Attack path — the highest-risk combination, read left to right as one AND ?Why this compound: privilege is only as strong as the credential in front of it, and only as contained as the gate in front of that. A permanent write role means there is no activation step to notice or approve; Tier-0 means the role is GA-class; weak or no MFA means the credential is phishable. Together that is one phish away from the top of the directory.

How to read it: left to right as one AND. The big number is how many accounts carry that factor and every factor before it, so it can only fall; the small number is how many carry that factor anywhere in the tenant. Colour is the severity the chain has reached; a green zero means the AND leaves nobody behind. Disabled accounts are excluded — they cannot sign in. Click a card to scope the table. hide ▴

Enterprise Applications

Security exposure·

Attack path — the highest-risk combination, read left to right as one AND ?Why this compound: a multi-tenant app registered in another tenant, holding application permissions, is app-only access with no signed-in user, no Conditional Access and no session to expire — on a credential that lives in the publisher’s tenant and that you cannot rotate. A high-privilege scope and an unverified publisher are the escalators on top of that.

How to read it: left to right as one AND. The big number is how many apps carry that factor and every factor before it, so it can only fall; the small number is how many hold that factor anywhere in the tenant. Colour is the severity the chain has reached; a green zero means the AND leaves nobody behind. Disabled service principals are excluded — they cannot authenticate, so their key is dormant. Click a card to scope the table to its cohort. hide ▴
